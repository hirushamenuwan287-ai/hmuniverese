OUR COSMIC NEIGHBORHOOD

Our Solar System

The Solar System contains the Sun and the objects that orbit it, including eight planets, moons, asteroids and comets.

Mercury

The smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun.

Venus

A hot planet with a thick atmosphere dominated by carbon dioxide.

Earth

Our home planet and the only world currently known to support life.

Mars

The red planet and one of the most explored worlds beyond Earth.

Jupiter

The largest planet in our Solar System.

Saturn

Famous for its spectacular system of rings.

Uranus

An ice giant that rotates with an extreme axial tilt.

Neptune

The farthest recognized planet from the Sun.

STELLAR WORLD

Stars

Stars are enormous balls of extremely hot plasma. They produce energy through nuclear fusion in their cores.

How are stars born?

Stars form inside huge clouds of gas and dust called molecular clouds. Gravity causes material to collapse until pressure and temperature become high enough for nuclear fusion to begin.

GIANT COSMIC STRUCTURES

Galaxies

Galaxies are enormous systems containing stars, planets, gas, dust and other matter held together by gravity.

Spiral Galaxies

Spiral galaxies have beautiful rotating arms extending from their central regions.

Elliptical Galaxies

These galaxies have smooth, rounded shapes and generally contain older stars.

Irregular Galaxies

These galaxies do not have a simple regular shape.

THE MYSTERIOUS UNIVERSE

Black Holes

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity is so strong that nothing, including light, can escape once it crosses the event horizon.

What is an Event Horizon?

The event horizon is the boundary around a black hole beyond which escape is impossible.

HUMANITY BEYOND EARTH

Space Exploration

Humans and robotic spacecraft have explored the Moon, planets, asteroids and distant regions of our Solar System.

Moon Exploration

The Moon was the first world beyond Earth visited by humans.

Mars Missions

Robotic missions have studied the Martian surface, atmosphere and geology.

Deep Space

Spacecraft have travelled far beyond the outer planets and continue exploring deep space.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HM Universe?

HM Universe is an educational website about space, astronomy, planets, stars and galaxies.

How many planets are in our Solar System?

There are eight officially recognized planets in our Solar System.

What is a galaxy?

A galaxy is a huge gravitationally bound system containing stars, gas, dust and other matter.

What is a black hole?

A black hole is an extremely dense region of spacetime with gravity strong enough to prevent light from escaping after crossing its event horizon.
